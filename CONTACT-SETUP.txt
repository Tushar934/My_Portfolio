PORTFOLIO CONTACT FORM

The message form is configured to POST to https://formspree.io/f/xbggqavv. The personal recipient email is not included in the website; configure and verify it privately in your Formspree dashboard.

PUBLISH AND VERIFY
1. Upload index.html, styles.css, script.js, and logo.svg to your GitHub Pages repository. This local update does not publish itself.
2. Check that your Formspree form is active and that the receiving mailbox is verified. Review allowed-domain settings, spam controls, usage limits, privacy, and retention settings in your dashboard.
3. Submit a non-sensitive test message from the published site. Confirm Formspree's response and actual receipt in your inbox. Check reply-to configuration. Delivery has not been verified until receipt is confirmed.

Submission sends the visitor's name, email, subject, and message to Formspree and navigates to its response page. The form uses native HTML POST and browser-required-field/email validation, so it also works without JavaScript. JavaScript adds whitespace trimming and endpoint validation. No live submission was made during local testing.

To change the service, update the contact-form action attribute in index.html to the new public HTTPS endpoint. Never place a recipient email, secret, or dashboard URL in this attribute. No mailto fallback exposes the recipient address.
